Food delivery apps

Seven apps for one food delivery company. Open any app below.

  • Food delivery app
    The customer app. Browse restaurants, order food and track the order.
  • Rider app
    The rider app. Go online, accept an order, pick it up and deliver it.
  • Restaurant app
    The restaurant tablet. Accept orders, mark them ready, and see the menu and payouts.
  • Ops tool
    The operations tool. Watch live orders, assign riders and check restaurant payouts.
  • Support tool
    The support tool. Answer customer tickets, link orders and send refunds.
  • KAM tool
    The key account manager tool. Manage restaurants, new sign-ups, requests and discounts.
  • Marketing tool
    The marketing tool. Manage pop-ups, push messages, promo codes and banners.
